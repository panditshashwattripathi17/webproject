Which length should I pick?

  • 25 min: classic Pomodoro. Good for starting hard tasks or when your focus is low.
  • 50 min: good for lectures, readings and problem sets.
  • 1 hr 30 min: deep work like debugging a big program. Take a 15-minute break after.
  • Short break is 5 minutes; after long sessions you'll get a longer break automatically.
  • Your chosen length and today's session count are saved on your account.
  • The timer shows in the browser tab, so you can keep it in the background.
